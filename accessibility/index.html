Accessibility

CASH HARD CLUB wants everyone to be able to browse our pieces, find our events and buy from us.

Our goal

We are working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA for cashhardclub.com and the Cash Hard Club app. We have not yet completed a full audit, and some parts may not work well with every assistive technology.

What we do now

  • Product names, prices, sizes and descriptions are text, and buttons in the app have screen-reader labels.
  • In the app, product galleries say which images are photos and which are computer-generated renders, and short motion previews stay still when your device’s Reduce Motion setting is on.
  • The website turns off background video and animation when your device’s Reduce Motion setting is on.
  • Videos have no sound. The product text is always the full description of a piece.

Known limitations

  • Checkout, cart and tickets run on our store platform (Squarespace), which we don’t control.
  • Not every product image has a written description yet.
  • Accessibility at event venues depends on the venue. Ask us before you buy, and we’ll share what we know and check with the venue.

If something doesn’t work for you

Email danielwhite@cashhardclub.com or kalencole@cashhardclub.com with the page or app screen and what happened. We aim to reply within a few business days. If something stops you from ordering, tell us and we’ll help you complete your order another way.
