Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · Applies to the Cash Hard Club mobile app (iOS and Android) and cashhardclub.com

The short version.

  • The app has no accounts and asks for no personal details.
  • Drop Alerts are opt-in. Nothing is sent to our notification provider (OneSignal) until you first switch Drop Alerts on. When you do, OneSignal creates a device record identified by a random ID. It does not include your name, email address or phone number. If you later switch alerts off, the record is kept but marked unsubscribed and you get no notifications; you can ask us to delete it (section 6).

  • We do not sell your data, run ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Using the app is also covered by our Terms of Use.

2. What the app collects

Drop Alerts (push notifications)

Notifications are opt-in: we never send you one unless you switch Drop Alerts on, and switching them off in the app stops them. Nothing is sent to OneSignal, the service that delivers our notifications, until you first switch Drop Alerts on. At that point the app creates a device record with OneSignal, identified by a random ID, and asks your phone for notification permission. OneSignal receives and stores:

  • a push token and random device, subscription and user identifiers (needed to deliver a notification to your phone);
  • device type and model, operating-system version, app version, language, time zone and mobile carrier;
  • your device’s IP address and the country or region OneSignal estimates from it (OneSignal does not store IP addresses for users in the EU and UK);
  • session data (when and how often the app is opened, and for how long) and whether a notification was received or tapped;
  • whether Drop Alerts are on or off, the alert topics you pick in the app (for example “new drops” or “events”), and when you changed them.

We do not attach your name, email address, phone number or precise (GPS) location to this record, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. OneSignal processes this data on our behalf under its privacy policy.

Saved pieces and preferences

Pieces you save are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. Your alert on/off choice and topics are also stored on your device and, once you turn alerts on, shared with OneSignal as described above.

Catalog, events and app updates

The app loads product and event information from cashhardclub.com and our Squarespace store, loads product images and short product videos from our content delivery network (Amazon CloudFront), and may check our update service (Expo Application Services) for app updates. Like any internet request, these include your device’s IP address and basic technical details (platform and app version); the update service also uses a random installation identifier. We do not use this information to identify you. The app may also send basic error or diagnostic information to the update service.

What we do not collect

No contacts, photos, microphone, precise location, advertising identifiers, or health data. The app contains no advertising or social-media tracking software.

3. Purchases on the official store

Everything we sell is physical merchandise or tickets to real-world events. When you tap “Buy” or “Get tickets”, the app opens our official store in a secure in-app browser window (Safari View Controller on iOS, Chrome Custom Tabs on Android). The store is operated by Squarespace and its checkout is subject to Squarespace’s privacy policy. As the merchant, we receive the order details you enter (name, shipping address, email, phone number and what you bought) so we can fulfil and support your order. Payment card details go directly to the payment processor and are never seen by us or by the app.

6. Your choices, retention and deletion

  • Turn alerts off: in the app, open the Alerts tab and switch Drop Alerts off. You can also disable notifications for the app in your phone’s Settings. Either stops all alerts.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes everything stored on your device and stops all notifications. The OneSignal device record is not removed automatically; see “Delete your data” below.
  • Delete your data: turning Drop Alerts off stops all notifications, but OneSignal keeps the device record until it is deleted. To have it deleted, tap “Delete my alert data” in the app’s Alerts tab (this emails us your device’s alert ID), or email danielwhite@cashhardclub.com with the alert ID shown there. We don’t hold your name or email in the alert system, so the ID is how we find your record. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. We also periodically delete records that have been inactive for a long time. Order records from the store are kept as long as required for accounting, tax and returns.
