CASH HARD CLUB

Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026 · Applies to the Cash Hard Club mobile app (iOS and Android) and cashhardclub.com

The short version.

  • The app has no accounts and asks for no personal details.
  • If you turn on Drop Alerts, our notification provider (OneSignal) stores a push token and a device identifier so we can deliver alerts. You can turn alerts off in the app at any time.
  • Purchases happen on our official store, run by Squarespace, in a browser window inside the app. The app never sees your payment details.
  • We do not sell your data, run ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

1. Who we are

CASH HARD CLUB (“we”, “us”) is a streetwear and club brand. Questions about this policy or your data: danielwhite@cashhardclub.com or kalencole@cashhardclub.com.

2. What the app collects

Drop Alerts (push notifications) — only if you turn them on

Alerts are opt-in. When you switch them on, the app asks your phone for notification permission and registers with OneSignal, the service that delivers our notifications. OneSignal receives and stores:

  • a push token and a random device/subscription identifier (needed to deliver a notification to your phone);
  • device type, operating-system version, app version, language and time zone;
  • basic interaction data: whether the app was opened, and whether a notification was received or tapped;
  • the alert topics you pick in the app (for example “new drops” or “events”).

We do not attach your name, email address, phone number or precise location to this record, and we do not use it to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. OneSignal processes this data on our behalf under its privacy policy.

Saved pieces and preferences

Pieces you save and your alert preferences are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us.

Catalog, events and app updates

The app loads product and event information from cashhardclub.com and our Squarespace store, and may check our update service (Expo Application Services) for app updates. Like any internet request, these include your device’s IP address and basic technical details (platform and app version); the update service also uses an anonymous installation identifier. We do not use this information to identify you.

What we do not collect

No contacts, photos, microphone, precise location, advertising identifiers, or health data. The app contains no advertising or social-media tracking software.

3. Purchases on the official store

Everything we sell is physical merchandise or tickets to real-world events. When you tap “Buy” or “Get tickets”, the app opens our official store in a secure in-app browser window (Safari View Controller on iOS, Chrome Custom Tabs on Android). The store is operated by Squarespace and its checkout is subject to Squarespace’s privacy policy. As the merchant, we receive the order details you enter (name, shipping address, email, phone number and what you bought) so we can fulfil and support your order. Payment card details go directly to the payment processor and are never seen by us or by the app.

4. How we use information

  • to deliver the Drop Alerts you asked for;
  • to understand, in aggregate, whether alerts are received and opened;
  • to fulfil orders placed on the store and answer support requests;
  • to keep the app working, secure and up to date.

6. Your choices, retention and deletion

  • Turn alerts off: in the app, open the Alerts tab and switch Drop Alerts off. You can also disable notifications for the app in your phone’s Settings. Either stops all alerts.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes everything stored on your device and stops all notifications.
  • Delete your data: push-subscription records are kept only while alerts are on and are removed when the subscription is unsubscribed or after a period of inactivity. Email us at danielwhite@cashhardclub.com from any address and we will delete any notification data linked to your device within 30 days. Order records are kept as long as required for accounting, tax and returns.

7. Children

The app and store are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our services uses HTTPS. The app does not store passwords or payment details. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the limited information we handle.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including California and the European Economic Area), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise any right, email us; we will respond within the time required by law and will never discriminate against you for making a request.

10. The website

cashhardclub.com is a static website hosted by Netlify. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and media from our content delivery network; those providers receive your IP address when the files are requested. The website sets no cookies of its own. The store on shopcashhardclub.squarespace.com uses cookies required for the shopping cart and checkout, governed by Squarespace’s policy.
