Orders & shipping

Every order is placed on our official store (Squarespace), which the app opens in a secure in-app browser. Your order confirmation and shipping updates arrive by email from the store. For anything about an order — status, address changes, returns — email us with your order number.

Sizing

Available sizes are listed on each piece in the app and chosen at checkout. Most tees run true to size with a relaxed fit; the hoodie is an oversized fit. If you are between sizes, email us before ordering and we’ll help.

Drop Alerts

Turning alerts on

  1. Open the app and tap Alerts.
  2. Switch Drop alerts on and allow notifications when your phone asks.
  3. Pick the topics you want: new drops, events & tickets.

Turning alerts off

Switch Drop alerts off in the Alerts tab, or turn off notifications for Cash Hard Club in your phone’s Settings. Either stops all alerts immediately.

Not receiving alerts?

  • Check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone’s Settings.
  • Focus / Do Not Disturb modes can silence alerts.
  • Open the app once after installing so it can register for alerts.

Events & tickets

Tickets are sold through the official store’s Event Tickets section. Your ticket confirmation arrives by email from the store; bring it (on your phone is fine) to the door. Age limits, if any, are shown on the event in the app.

Your data

The app has no accounts and stores your saved pieces only on your phone. To delete any notification data linked to your device, email us — we delete it within 30 days. Details are in our privacy policy.

App problems

If something looks wrong or the app crashes, email us with your phone model, iOS or Android version, and what you tapped. Screenshots help. Updates are published through the App Store and Google Play.
